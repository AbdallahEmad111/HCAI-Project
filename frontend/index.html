About AI Research Roadmap Agent & Limits

  • What I am: An explainable AI assistant built for CS, SC, AI, and IS researchers.
  • Knowledge base: 12 verified open-access (CC BY 4.0) papers from 2025–2026 (planned expansion: 32 papers) and 3 verified publication venues.
  • How Explainability works: Python + SQLite FTS5 computes skill overlap, topic relevance, and hardware feasibility first. Gemini only phrases the summary from those retrieved papers.
  • What I cannot do: I cannot confirm 100% novelty, guarantee journal acceptance, invent citations, or answer questions outside CS/SC/AI/IS.
  • Keyboard shortcuts: Use Tab to navigate controls, Enter to submit, and the top bar to scale text up to 200%.
